# Card art prompts

One prompt per skill. Copy a block into ChatGPT, then paste the picture it makes into the Claude chat with the skill name.
The card builder adds the frame, header, number, title and tagline on top of the picture, and
takes the card's colours from it.

**What to send back:** paste the picture into the Claude chat (any format), plus which skill it's for.

**Check before sending:** no words or letters anywhere in the picture, and the subject sits in
the middle (the top and bottom get covered).

---

## 01 · Code Audit

```text
Portrait illustration, 1024 x 1536.

Scene: an inspector with a lantern and a giant magnifying glass examining the gears of an enormous clockwork machine, tying small red flags to a few faulty gears.

Colour palette: deep teal + safety yellow. One bold dominant colour plus the accent.

Style: rich, textured illustration, like a collectible card game card.

Composition:
- Full-bleed: the background fills the entire image, edge to edge.
- Keep the main subject in the middle of the image.
- Top 15%: plain background only. A header will cover it.
- Bottom 30%: plain background only. A title band will cover it.

Rules:
- NO text, letters, numbers, labels or signs anywhere in the image.
- NO border, frame or card shape. Just the illustration.
- No real people, brands, logos or recognisable products.
```
