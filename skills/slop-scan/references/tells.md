# Visual AI Tells

Mark each: Present / Partial / Absent / Not checked.

## Color
- **Gradients**: purple, indigo, violet, blue-to-purple washes; gradient buttons; gradient section backgrounds.
- **Gradient text** on headings.
- **Glow orbs**: blurred colored circles behind the hero.
- **Purple glow on near-black** dark mode.
- **One accent color** that could be swapped for any other without anything changing.

## Type
- **Highlighted heading word**: one word or phrase set in a script font (Caveat, Dancing Script, Pacifico, Great Vibes, Kalam), italic serif, a different color, gradient, or with a hand-drawn underline/circle.
- **Em dashes** used as decoration across headings, subheads, buttons and cards.
- **Inter or Geist** everywhere, one weight doing all the work, hierarchy by size only.
- **Huge tight-tracked headline**, centered.
- **ALL-CAPS tracked eyebrow labels** above headings.
- **"A · B · C" middle-dot strings** and "→" on every link or button.
- **Emoji** in headings, bullets or buttons (🚀 ✨ ⚡).

## Shape and surface
- **Excessive rounding**: most boxes, images and buttons at 12px+ radius, one radius everywhere.
- **Hairline borders** on every card and panel, plus a soft shadow.

## Components
- **Eyebrow pill** above the hero headline ("✨ New: …").
- **Solid + ghost button pair** under the headline.
- **Stat row**: three big numbers in a row.
- **Icon tiles**: line icon in a tinted rounded square above each card heading.
- **Identical cards**: rows of three, same size, same radius, hairline border, soft shadow.
- **Left accent bar** on cards.
- **Bento grid**: unequal tiles with one radius and one hairline.
- **"Most popular" middle pricing card**, scaled up.
- **Round-avatar testimonial row** with five gold stars each.
- **FAQ accordion** closing the page.
- **Checkmark rows**: four or more identical lines with check icons.
- **Logo marquee** of gray logos.
- **Monogram logo**: a letter in a rounded square.

## Mechanical layout
- **Same gap between every section.**
- **Everything centered in one max-width column.** Nothing full-bleed, cropped, overlapping or off-grid.
- **Alternating white / light-gray section backgrounds.**
- **One radius, one border, one shadow on everything.**
- **Same section order as every AI site**: hero → logos → 3 features → alternating text/image rows → testimonials → pricing → FAQ → big CTA.
- **Mobile is just desktop stacked** into one column.

## Imagery (visual treatment only, never authenticity)
- **AI-rendered look**: waxy skin, too-perfect lighting, glassy 3D objects, abstract blobs and meshes.
- **No imagery at all**, just icons and shapes.
- **Dark-overlay hero**: full-bleed photo, dark gradient overlay, white centered headline.

## Motion and effects
- **Fade-up on every section** as you scroll.
- **Hover lift + shadow grow** on every card.
- **Glassmorphism**: frosted blurred panels, blurred sticky nav.
- **Glowing or shimmering buttons.**

## Clusters
- **v0 / shadcn kit**: white or zinc, Inter/Geist, rounded-lg cards with hairline borders, Lucide icons, outline + solid buttons, muted gray text.
- **Lovable / Bolt purple SaaS**: indigo-to-violet gradients, gradient headline text, glow blob behind the hero, eyebrow pill, three feature cards with icon tiles.
- **Dark glow SaaS**: near-black background, purple or blue glow, glass panels, gradient text.
- **Claude-style cream editorial**: warm cream background (~#F4F1EA), serif display, terracotta accent (~#D97757), italic accent words, tracked caps labels.
- **Acid on black**: near-black with one acid-green or vermilion accent, mono labels, giant type.
- **Bento product page**: bento grid with a fake dashboard tile, "Most popular" pricing, avatar testimonial row, FAQ accordion.
