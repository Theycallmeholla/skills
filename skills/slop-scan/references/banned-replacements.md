# Banned Replacement Moves

When an agent is told "make it look less AI", it reaches for these. Each is just the next AI tell. Don't use them as fixes, and flag them in the re-audit if a fix pass introduced them.

"It adds visual interest" is not a reason to use one.

## Layout moves
- Asymmetric hero with a card or stat box overlapping the photo.
- Giant outlined (`-webkit-text-stroke`) or ghosted word behind the headline.
- Oversized watermark numbers ("01", "25", "since 1998") behind sections.
- Generic "break the grid": an element nudged off-column with no content reason.
- Split hero with a rounded-corner photo on the right and a floating badge on the photo corner.
- Diagonal or wavy section dividers.

## Type moves
- One word in the headline set in a script font, italic serif or a different color.
- Swapping Inter for another safe grotesk (Manrope, Plus Jakarta Sans, DM Sans, Satoshi, General Sans) with nothing else changed.
- Serif display + sans body chosen only to look "editorial".
- Mono labels for small data.

## Color moves
- Purple → cream + terracotta + serif.
- Purple → near-black + acid green or vermilion.
- Purple → black and white "minimal" with one accent.

## Decoration moves
- Marquee of service names or keywords.
- Grain/noise overlay, film texture, or "paper" background added for warmth.
- Hand-drawn underline or circle SVG around a headline word.
- Hairline rules and numbered sections added to feel crafted.
- Sticker/badge rotated a few degrees ("Est. 2009!").

## Photo moves
- Duotone or color-tint overlay on photos.
- Full-bleed photo with dark overlay and white centered headline.
- Rounded-corner photo with a colored offset block behind it.
